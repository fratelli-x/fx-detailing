Our Philosophy: A Lifetime of Excellence

Led by Reece Baughan, who has been personally responsible for managing, restoring, and maintaining millions of pounds worth of prestige automotive inventory in showroom condition for over 10 years.

Our company values are simple: We are looking to align with clients who want their vehicles returned to and maintained in showroom condition. To achieve this, we have developed a strategic maintenance cycle that ensures your vehicle never degrades, while making world-class care surprisingly affordable. By trusting us as the long-term guardians of your car, we make sure that “new car” feeling never fades away.

A Decade Spent at the Pinnacle of Automotive Excellence.

For 10+ years our hands have touched the paintwork of the world’s most extraordinary vehicles - from the raw performance of Ferrari, McLaren and Lamborghini to the timeless elegance of Rolls-Royce and Porsche.
