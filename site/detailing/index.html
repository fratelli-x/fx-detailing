The FX Standard: A Level of Care Beyond the Ordinary

Detailing – From £299

The Showroom Reset. This service returns your vehicle to its original factory condition. It is a comprehensive top-to-bottom cleanse that includes a deep interior and exterior detail, engine bay detail, premium dressings for trims, plastics and tyres.

Breakdown: Exterior Detail + Interior Detail + Engine Bay Detail + Premium Dressings

Note: This is a cleansing service - consider adding ceramic protection or machine polishing.

Custom & Partial Service: If you are looking for a specific, isolated treatment - like individual stain removal or an interior only detail - get in touch with us today for a custom quote.

Paint enhancement – From £299

Instant Gloss Recovery. Designed for vehicles with light swirl marks or dull paint. This single-stage machine polish removes the “dull” layer of the clear coat, restoring optical clarity and brings the paint back to life. It’s the perfect entry-level service for those seeking a dramatic improvement in gloss without the full correction timeframe.

Breakdown: Exterior Detail + Decontamination + Single Stage Machine Polishing

Note: Add an interior detail for an extra £100

Paint correction – From £1,699

Ultimate Paint Restoration. This is our highest level of paint correction, designed for vehicles with heavy swirl marks, scratches, oxidation, holograms, and significant paint defects. Using a meticulous three-stage machine polishing process, we restore depth, clarity, and gloss, achieving a 90%+ correction factor.

Breakdown: Exterior Detail + Decontamination + Three-Stage Machine Polishing

Note: Paint correction removes defects within the clear coat, but some deeper scratches and stone chips may remain if they have penetrated beyond the safe polishing limit.

Ideal For: Enthusiasts, collectors, and owners who want their vehicle returned to the best possible condition.

Ceramic protection – From £799

The Shield. Ceramic coating creates a molecular bond with the paint, adds gloss, reduces new swirl marks, repels dirt, contaminants and water.

Breakdown: Exterior Detail + Decontamination + Premium Exterior Ceramic Coating

Note: Investment may vary if you require interior ceramic protection as well as if you have larger vehicles such as SUVs.

Maintenance detailing – From £100

Preserve the Perfection. A specialised service designed exclusively for vehicles we have already detailed. This ensures your car maintains its showroom condition all-year-round.

Breakdown: Exterior Detail + Interior Detail

Specialist services & add-ons

Bespoke Finishing Touches. Beyond our primary packages, we offer specialised treatments to address specific areas of concern. These services can be booked individually or added to any detailing package for a total vehicle transformation.

  • New Car Detail From £999 - A comprehensive new vehicle preparation service including a single-stage machine polish to refine factory paintwork, followed by premium ceramic protection for long-term gloss, clarity, and durability.
  • Vehicle Pre-Sale Detail From £499 - A complete presentation package designed to maximise resale value with a full interior and exterior detail, engine bay cleaning, decontamination, and premium dressings. Optional enhancement polishing available for improved gloss and presentation.
  • Interior Ceramic Protection £300 - Protects surfaces by repelling spills, resisting stains, and reducing wear and tear, typically maintaining an OEM matte look without being greasy.
  • Headlight Restoration £80 - We remove oxidation, yellowing, and light pitting from UV-damaged lenses. This multi-stage wet-sanding and polishing process restores crystal-clear optical clarity and improves nighttime visibility.
  • Engine Bay Detailing From £80 - A deep-clean of the heart of your vehicle. We safely remove grease and grime before applying heat-resistant, non-sticky dressings to all plastics and hoses for a factory-fresh appearance.
  • Full Paint Decontamination from £200 – A three-stage chemical and mechanical process (Iron/Tar/Clay Bar) to remove embedded road grime and fallout. Note: Once decontaminated, we recommend a single-stage machine polish and ceramic coating to lock in the finish.
  • Paintless Dent Removal / PDR From £80 - We eliminate small dings and “trolley dents” without the need for fillers or paint. This specialist technique preserves your original factory paintwork and structural integrity.
  • Convertible Roof Restoration & Protection £400 - Fabric roofs are magnets for moss, algae, and industrial fallout. Our process begins with a multi-stage deep clean to lift ingrained contaminants from the fibres. To restore a factory-fresh appearance, we repaint the fabric to bring back deep, uniform colour to faded tops. Finally, we apply a dedicated ceramic fabric protection that repels water, reduces UV degradation, and prevents future moss and algae growth.
