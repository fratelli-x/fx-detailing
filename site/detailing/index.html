Bespoke finishing touches

Specialist services

Beyond our primary packages, we offer specialised treatments to address specific areas of concern. These services can be booked individually or added to any detailing package for a total vehicle transformation.

  • New Car Detail From £999 — A comprehensive new vehicle preparation service including a single-stage machine polish to refine factory paintwork, followed by premium ceramic protection for long-term gloss, clarity, and durability.
  • Vehicle Pre-Sale Detail From £499 — A complete presentation package designed to maximise resale value with a full interior and exterior detail, engine bay cleaning, decontamination, and premium dressings. Optional enhancement polishing available for improved gloss and presentation.
  • Interior Ceramic Protection £300 — Protects surfaces by repelling spills, resisting stains, and reducing wear and tear, typically maintaining an OEM matte look without being greasy.
  • Headlight Restoration £80 — We remove oxidation, yellowing, and light pitting from UV-damaged lenses. This multi-stage wet-sanding and polishing process restores crystal-clear optical clarity and improves nighttime visibility.
  • Engine Bay Detailing From £80 — A deep-clean of the heart of your vehicle. We safely remove grease and grime before applying heat-resistant, non-sticky dressings to all plastics and hoses for a factory-fresh appearance.
  • Full Paint Decontamination from £200 — A three-stage chemical and mechanical process (Iron/Tar/Clay Bar) to remove embedded road grime and fallout. Once decontaminated, we recommend a single-stage machine polish and ceramic coating to lock in the finish.
  • Paintless Dent Removal / PDR From £80 — We eliminate small dings and “trolley dents” without the need for fillers or paint. This specialist technique preserves your original factory paintwork and structural integrity.
  • Convertible Roof Restoration & Protection £400 — Fabric roofs are magnets for moss, algae, and industrial fallout. Our process begins with a multi-stage deep clean to lift ingrained contaminants from the fibres. To restore a factory-fresh appearance, we repaint the fabric to bring back deep, uniform colour to faded tops. Finally, we apply a dedicated ceramic fabric protection that repels water, reduces UV degradation, and prevents future moss and algae growth.
  • Sell Your Vehicle via Fratelli X — Leverage our decade of luxury automotive sales experience. We can manage the entire sale of your vehicle on a commission basis, including all detailing, professional media and advertising at no upfront cost to you. Submit your sales enquiry here.
