Need to know

FAQs

Everything you need to know before protecting your vehicle.

We work with most makes and models, from daily drivers to luxury, performance and supercars. We will confirm the best treatment for your vehicle before booking.

Send us an enquiry with your vehicle details and preferred service. Once we have confirmed availability, we will guide you through the booking process.

Most ceramic coating applications take between two and five days, depending on the vehicle, preparation required and the protection package selected.

Durability depends on the product and maintenance. Our Gtechniq coatings can provide protection for up to five years when correctly applied and maintained.

Preparation is tailored to the condition of your vehicle. Paint correction can be included where needed, and we will explain any additional preparation before work begins.

Ceramic coating chemically bonds to the surface, providing stronger, longer-lasting protection and easier maintenance than traditional wax.

The hydrophobic surface helps repel water, dirt and contaminants, so your vehicle stays cleaner for longer and takes less effort to maintain.

Ceramic coating helps resist light contamination and chemical damage, but it is not scratch-proof or a replacement for paint protection film.

It protects your finish from UV exposure, road contamination and chemical fallout while enhancing gloss and making regular cleaning easier.

Gtechniq products offer proven performance, excellent clarity and a complete range of protection systems for paint, wheels, glass, trim and interiors.
