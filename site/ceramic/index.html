Why most cars lose their showroom finish

Cars don’t get scratched when they get dirty.
They get scratched when you wash them.

Ceramic coating changes that.

Over time, correcting these issues becomes significantly more expensive, or even impossible, compared with preventing them in the first place.

Without protection, paintwork becomes:

  • Harder to clean
  • More prone to swirl marks
  • Vulnerable to permanent water spot damage
  • Dull from sun damage
  • Prone to paint bubbling and rust
  • Increasingly difficult to maintain

Professional Gtechniq Ceramic Coatings & Paint Protection

Why We Trust Gtechniq Systems for Prestige Vehicles

FX Detailing works independently and is not claiming a Gtechniq accreditation or badge. We use Gtechniq products with multi-stage machine polishing and preparation for gloss, durability, and hydrophobic protection.

Crystal Serum Light

A ceramic coating for paintwork. It bonds to the clear coat, adds gloss, and resists swirl marks, wash chemicals, bird droppings and water spots. Gtechniq rates it for up to five years when it is looked after.

C5 Wheel Armour

A ceramic coating for wheels, brake calipers and exhaust tips. It bonds to the surface, repels brake dust and road dirt, and withstands heat up to 600°C. Gtechniq rates it for up to two years on gloss, satin and matte finishes.

HALO flexible film coating

Made for paint protection film and vinyl, including gloss, satin and matte. It bonds to the film, sheds dirt and water, and helps reduce UV yellowing. Gtechniq rates it for up to two years.

G1 ClearVision Smart Glass

A hydrophobic coating that chemically bonds to glass. Rain beads and rolls off, which keeps the view clearer in wet weather. Gtechniq rates it for up to 20,000 miles.

C4 Permanent Trim Restorer

Restores faded exterior plastic, vinyl and rubber to an as-new finish without a greasy shine. It bonds to the trim and Gtechniq rates the protection for up to two years.

L1 Leather Guard

A matte, abrasion-resistant finish for leather. It helps prevent dye transfer, UV fade and everyday wear, and Gtechniq states treated surfaces kill 99.9% of bacteria. Durability is 3 to 12 months, depending on wear.

I1 Smart Fabric

A breathable coating for cloth seats, carpets, mats and soft tops. It repels water and oil-based spills, includes anti-mould protection, and lasts up to 12 months without changing how the fabric feels.

FAQs

Do you work on every car?

FX Detailing works primarily with luxury, classic, performance and exotic vehicles. This offer is open to owners who genuinely want to protect and preserve their vehicle properly.

How do I secure my booking?

Submit an enquiry through the form below and we will contact you to discuss your vehicle and availability.

How long does it take?

The job can be completed within 3–5 days. We keep the vehicle in our indoor facility overnight after the ceramic application for the best result.

How long does a professional ceramic coating application take?

Due to the extensive multi-stage paint correction and preparation required to achieve a flawless finish on prestige vehicles, our ceramic coating packages typically take between 2 to 4 days.

How long does ceramic coating last?

Up to 5 years, whether the car is garage kept or used for normal daily driving.

Is paint correction included?

Paint correction is a separate full vehicle restoration. It is added only if needed, and in most cases takes about 7 days when combined with this offer.

How is ceramic different from wax?

Waxes sit temporarily on the surface and degrade quickly. Professional ceramic coatings bond directly to the paintwork, with greater durability, better gloss and multi-year protection.

Will my vehicle stay cleaner?

Yes. Protected surfaces become significantly more hydrophobic, so water, grime and contaminants struggle to bond to the paintwork. The car is easier to clean and stays cleaner for longer.

Does ceramic coating stop scratches?

Ceramic coating helps reduce light scratches, swirl marks and wash-induced marring. It is not a substitute for paint protection film, and it cannot prevent deep scratches or stone chips.

Why is ceramic coating important?

Without protection, moisture, road contamination and environmental fallout sit against the paintwork. Over time that contributes to dullness, sun damage, swirl marks, corrosion, premature ageing and water spots. Ceramic coating is a long-term barrier between the vehicle and the environment.

Why do you use Gtechniq?

Gtechniq is recognised for professional-grade ceramic protection trusted by high-end detailers. We use it for durability, gloss, hydrophobic performance and long-term reliability.
