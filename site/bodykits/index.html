Why owners trust FX Detailing

  • We install kits from Maxton Design, CT Carbon, Xclusive Customz and other manufacturers to the same standard.
  • Every car is dry-fitted so alignment is right before anything is fixed.
  • We can also apply ceramic coating protection to new carbon fibre splitters, diffusers, and bodykits prior to fitment to guard against stone chips and UV degradation.
  • We use factory mounting points and automotive-grade adhesives so original panels stay intact.
  • We do not cut or permanently alter factory metalwork.
  • Installations are planned so the car can be returned to factory if you choose.
