The Review

There are moments in the life of a business that clarify why you started it. A client's words that confirm, without any room for doubt, that the standard you have spent years building is exactly what you thought it was.

Zak Brown, CEO of McLaren Formula 1, is one of the most significant figures in world motorsport. He has access to the finest automotive services available anywhere on earth. His standards are not ordinary standards - they are the standards of someone for whom automotive excellence is a professional baseline, not an aspiration.

He has been trusting FX Detailing with his personal vehicles for a number of years.

In his own words: he has never been happier with the results. He describes our attention to detail as second to none. He says we consistently go above and beyond. And he says he would not use anyone else.

What This Means in Practice

Zak Brown's cars do not come to our Charlwood studio. We go to him. The mobile detailing service that allows us to work on a £2 million Porsche Carrera GT at a client's private driveway in Surrey is the same service that allows us to travel to Zak and deliver the complete FX Detailing standard at his location, with the same equipment, the same products and the same obsessive attention to every detail.

The standard does not change with the location. It does not change with the client. It does not change with the car.

That consistency - across ten years, across every vehicle, across every visit - is what has produced a record of 100% exceptional feedback. Not because we promise perfection. Because we will not hand a car back until it is.

The Standard That Earns This Kind of Trust

When someone in Zak Brown's position chooses a detailing service and stays with it for years, something specific is being confirmed: that the standard is not the result of a good day or a particularly careful visit. It is the baseline. It is what always happens, not what sometimes happens.

Building that consistency requires operating at a deliberately low volume. It requires accepting fewer cars than we could fill a diary with - so that every car we do accept receives the undivided attention it deserves. It requires working only with people who understand the difference between a car that looks clean and a car that has been finished to its genuine potential.

We have never had an unhappy client. Ten years. Not a single exception.

If you own a vehicle that deserves this standard - whether it is a McLaren, a Porsche, a Ferrari, a Rolls-Royce or any other car you genuinely care about - we would be honoured to add it to our record.
