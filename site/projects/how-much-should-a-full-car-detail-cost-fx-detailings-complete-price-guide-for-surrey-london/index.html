What Is Actually Included in a Full Detail?

A full detail is not a valet. The word is used loosely across the industry, so it is worth being specific about what a genuine full detail involves at FX Detailing.

A full detail covers the complete exterior and interior of the car. On the exterior: a two-stage decontamination process (pre-wash snow foam, pH-neutral contact wash, iron fallout removal and clay bar decontamination). Every panel gap, crevice, rubber seal, moss & algae, every glass surface, every wheel arch, engine bay and every door shut.

On the interior: every surface addressed individually. Leather cleaned and conditioned. Alcantara surfaces - steering wheels, headlining, seats, carpets, floor mats, dashboard, centre console, buttons, levers, gear stick, pedals - cleaned with dedicated products and groomed. Every hard surface, every vent, every switch. The glass cleaned streak-free inside and out.

A full detail without machine polishing provides for a complete vehicle cleanse. Not a single component is left unaddressed - this is what we call "The Showroom Reset". A full detail with machine polishing also corrects the paint - removing the swirl marks, fine scratches and imperfections that accumulate on any car over time and that are the difference between paint that looks good and paint that looks genuinely extraordinary.

Why Machine Polishing Changes the Price Significantly

Machine polishing "paint correction" is the most time-intensive, intricate and transformational service in professional luxury car detailing. On a typical car, a full multi-stage correction takes anywhere from a few days to a full week's worth of work: panel-by-panel assessment under specialist lighting, product selection for each individual surface, and the correction process itself carried out carefully enough to achieve genuine defect removal without burning through the paint.

The difference between corrected and uncorrected paint on a dark colour - Carbon Black, Santorini Black, deep navy - is not subtle. It is the difference between paint that looks flat and grey under direct light and paint that has that deep, wet-looking mirror gloss it was designed to have. On a car you care about, it is the service that makes the most visible difference to what you see every time you approach it.

The price reflects the time involved - and the expertise required to carry it out without damaging the paint surface in the process.

Why Supercars Cost More to Detail

The higher end of our pricing range - approaching £3,000 for a full detail, machine polishing and ceramic coating - applies to supercars and hypercars. Also applies to luxury cars that haven't been maintained well at all for which it will require for us to go through hell to get it looking good again. The reason is straightforward: these cars require more of everything.

More time - McLaren's carbon fibre bodywork requires a completely different approach to steel or aluminium panels. Ferrari's paint systems vary significantly across generations and must be understood specifically before any correction work begins. Lamborghini's clear coats are softer than most and require lighter, more careful correction.

More care - On a car worth £200,000 or £500,000 or more, paint thickness readings are taken across every panel before any abrasive product is applied. The margin for error is professionally and personally zero. The process is slower because it has to be.

More accountability - When the CEO of McLaren Formula 1 trusts us with his personal vehicles - as Zak Brown has done for a number of years - the standard being applied is not a standard that changes based on what the job is worth on paper. It is the same standard, on every car, without exception.

UK Car Detailing Pricing FAQ

How Much Does Professional Car Detailing Cost in the UK?

Professional detailing in the UK varies by the level of work required. A thorough full detail typically starts at £300 - £500, ceramic coating starts at around £500, while multi-stage paint correction for supercars can reach £3,000+ because the service usually takes a whole week to be completed.

Why is Specialist Car Detailing Expensive?

The cost reflects the time required and the quality of the end result. A full correction detail at our Surrey studio often takes around 7 full days. We call this service "Total Reset & Protection". You will never have to pay for this service again for as long as we are maintaining your cars.

Are Ceramic Coatings a Waste of Money?

No, especially for luxury vehicles. A professional-grade ceramic coating creates an impenetrable bond with the paint, protecting it from paint bubbling and rusting, environmental contaminants, water spots and UV damage. Also, significantly reducing the risk of swirl marks.

Which is Better: a Valet or a Full Detail?

 A valet is a basic-level wash. A correction detail is a restoration process. Simply put - valeting is a basic car wash and detailing is the process of returning a vehicle back to its showroom condition whether correction is needed or not.
