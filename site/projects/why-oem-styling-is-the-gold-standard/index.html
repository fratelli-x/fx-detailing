Why OEM+ Styling Is the Gold Standard for Modern Performance Cars

Why OEM+ Styling Has Become the Gold Standard for Modern Performance Cars

Walk through any dealership showroom today and you'll notice something interesting.

The most desirable performance cars rarely rely on exaggerated styling to stand out. Instead, manufacturers like Porsche, BMW, Mercedes-AMG and Lamborghini have spent decades refining subtle proportions, sharper aerodynamics and cleaner body lines that age gracefully over time.

It's no surprise that this same philosophy has become increasingly popular among enthusiasts.

Known as OEM+ styling, it's an approach to vehicle modification that focuses on enhancing the original design rather than replacing it.

At FX Detailing, it's a philosophy we strongly believe in, and one that influences many of the body kit installations we carry out.

What Does OEM+ Actually Mean?

OEM stands for Original Equipment Manufacturer, referring to the components originally designed and fitted by the vehicle manufacturer.

OEM+ isn't about leaving a vehicle completely standard.

Instead, it's about making thoughtful improvements that respect the manufacturer's original design language.

The aim is simple.

If someone unfamiliar with the vehicle saw it for the first time, they might assume it left the factory looking exactly that way.

That's often the highest compliment an installation can receive.

Why Less Is Often More

One of the biggest mistakes enthusiasts make is assuming that more modifications automatically create a better-looking car.

In reality, the opposite is often true.

Modern performance vehicles are designed by teams of engineers and designers who spend years refining every contour, vent and body line.

Adding oversized spoilers, poorly fitting splitters or aggressive styling components can quickly disrupt that balance.

OEM+ styling takes a more restrained approach.

Rather than dominating the original design, quality components complement it.

A carefully designed front splitter can visually lower a vehicle without appearing excessive.

A roof spoiler can extend the silhouette naturally.

A rear diffuser can introduce greater presence while maintaining factory proportions.

When every component works together, the overall result feels intentional rather than forced.

Choosing the Right Components Matters

Not every aftermarket body kit is created to the same standard.

While two products may appear similar in photographs, differences in manufacturing tolerances, material quality and vehicle-specific engineering often become obvious during installation.

High-quality components generally require less adjustment, integrate more naturally with factory bodywork and produce a cleaner finished appearance.

That's one of the reasons we work with carefully selected manufacturers whose products consistently meet the standards our customers expect.

Professional installation then ensures those components achieve the fitment and symmetry they were designed for.

Professional Installation Makes the Difference

The quality of a body kit isn't determined solely by the parts themselves.

Installation plays an equally important role.

Even premium components can look out of place if they're not aligned correctly.

Professional installation involves far more than attaching new parts to a vehicle.

Every component should be inspected, dry-fitted, aligned and evaluated from multiple angles before permanent installation begins.

The objective isn't simply to make the parts fit.

It's to make them appear as though they have always belonged on the vehicle.

OEM+ Is About Long-Term Ownership

One of the reasons OEM+ styling continues to grow in popularity is its longevity.

Unlike trend-driven modifications that quickly become dated, subtle enhancements often remain attractive for years because they're rooted in the vehicle's original design philosophy.

Many owners tell us they don't want a car that attracts attention for the wrong reasons.

They simply want the best possible version of the vehicle they already own.

That's exactly what OEM+ delivers.

A Recent Example

We recently completed a Mercedes-Benz A-Class W177 CT Carbon body kit installation, where the goal wasn't to dramatically alter the vehicle but to enhance its existing AMG styling with carefully selected aerodynamic components.

The result demonstrates exactly what OEM+ styling is all about a vehicle that looks sharper, lower and more purposeful while remaining unmistakably Mercedes.

If you haven't seen the full project, it's well worth reading as it explores the installation process, the reasoning behind the component selection and the importance of professional fitment.

Final Thoughts

At FX Detailing, we believe the most successful modifications are often the ones that don't immediately announce themselves.

They're the vehicles that simply look "right."

The proportions feel balanced.

The body lines flow naturally.

The styling enhances rather than distracts.

That's the essence of OEM+.

And it's a philosophy we'll continue to champion across every premium vehicle that enters our workshop.
