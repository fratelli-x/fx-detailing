Built from within Fratelli X - one of Surrey's most respected luxury car dealerships - our standards weren't invented in a marketing meeting. They were forged over ten years of working on some of the most extraordinary vehicles on the planet: Porsches, McLarens, Ferraris, Lamborghinis, Rolls-Royces and many others.

We exist because the owners of these cars deserve better than a generic valet service. A £200,000 supercar is not just a vehicle. It is an engineering masterpiece, a financial investment, and for most owners, a deeply personal statement. It deserves to be treated accordingly.

What We Do

FX Detailing specialises in two disciplines, both performed to the same uncompromising standard:

Luxury Car Detailing - from maintenance washes and machine polishing to full paint correction and professional ceramic coating, every service is carried out with surgical precision using the highest grade products available. We work on one car at a time, with full attention, until nothing more can be improved.

Bodykit Installation - as authorised dealers and installers for CT Carbon and Xclusive Customz, we fit everything from carbon splitters and rear diffusers to full body transformations. Every component is aligned to the millimetre, using factory mounting points wherever possible to preserve your car's structural integrity and resale value.

Why a Specialist Matters

Generic valeting services are simply not equipped to work on prestige and high-performance vehicles. The paint on a modern supercar requires specific compounds, specific machine speeds and a technician who understands the difference between a correction and an improvement. The wrong product on a matte finish, a ceramic-coated surface or a carbon fibre panel can cause damage that costs thousands to reverse.

At FX Detailing, every product we use and every technique we apply has been selected for the specific demands of luxury and performance vehicles. From the carbon fibre splitter on a modern hypercar to the delicate single-stage paintwork on a classic, we know exactly what each surface needs - and more importantly, what it doesn't.

Why is FX Detailing considered a supercar specialist?
