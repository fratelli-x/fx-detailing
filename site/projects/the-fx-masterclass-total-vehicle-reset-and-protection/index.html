The FX Total Reset & Protection: A Full Vehicle Reset, Protection and Maintenance for the World’s Finest Vehicles

Our company value is simple: We are looking to align with clients who want to maintain their vehicles in showroom condition all-year-round. We don't believe in temporary fixes - we believe in a lifetime of perfection. To achieve this, we have developed a strategic maintenance cycle that ensures your vehicle never degrades, while making world-class care surprisingly affordable. By trusting us as the long-term guardians of your car, we make sure that "New Car" feeling never fades away.

How We Maintain Your Investment:

The Total Reset (Once): We begin with a full detail and paint correction to establish a baseline. Because we do this so thoroughly, you will never need to pay for a "Total Reset" again as long as we look after your car.

Total Protection (Once): A ceramic coating is applied once every 5 years to add gloss, protect from swirl marks, repel dirt, water spots and UV damage.

The Refresh (Quarterly): Every few months, we refresh all interior and exterior dressings to keep plastics, trims, tyres and engine components looking factory-fresh.

The Maintenance (Monthly): For just £100 a month, we perform a professional Maintenance Detail. This keeps your vehicle in 365-day showroom condition and ensures the correction we performed is preserved indefinitely.

We are here to look after you and your most prized possession. When we manage the lifecycle of your car’s appearance, perfection becomes the standard, not the exception.

Breakdown of Our 7 Day Flagship  Package -  The FX Total Reset & Protection.

1. The Deconstruction of Dirt (Day 1-2)

Most detailing starts with a wash. We start with a search for every hidden imperfection.

The Crevice Detail: We make sure to remove organic moss, algae, and "gunk" from window rubbers, panel gaps, and door jambs.

The "Invisible" Areas: We deep-clean the areas most ignore: inside the fuel filler cap, under the wheel arches, the brake callipers, and the entire engine bay.

The Interior Reset: Air vents, seat rails, and every stitch of leather are meticulously detailed.

2. Permanent Paint Correction (Day 3-6)

We don't "hide" scratches with fillers that wash off in a month. We remove them permanently.

3-Stage Machine Polishing: We go through a methodical sequence of compounding, polishing, and jeweling.

The Goal: We restore optical clarity to the clear coat. This is what gives your paint that famous FX "Deep Colour Pop" and mirror-like saturation. Removing 95%+ of imperfections.

3. The Finishing Touches (Day 7)

Once the vehicle is mechanically and aesthetically perfect, we move to the final dressings.

The Factory Finish: We apply premium dressings to all plastics and trims - inside, outside, and under the hood.

The Leather Reset: All leather is detailed to restore its natural matte look and supple feel - no oily residues, just factory-fresh quality.

4. Ceramic Protection

For our Total Reset & Protection clients, the final days are dedicated to "locking in" the perfection.

Exterior Ceramic Shield: We apply premium ceramic protection to the entire exterior and windows.

Our Philosophy: A Lifetime of Excellence

Our goal is to restore and maintain your vehicle to the highest level for the entire duration of ownership. We treat every car as if it were entering the Fratelli X showroom for sale. By performing a Total Reset, we create a baseline of perfection that - when maintained correctly - preserves your vehicle to the highest level and maintains it in showroom condition at all times.
